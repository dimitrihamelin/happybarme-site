Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

In short

HappyNewBar does not collect, store or share any personal data. No account, no tracking, no analytics, no ads.

The app

  • HappyNewBar runs entirely on your Mac. It does not send any data to us or to third parties.
  • To show your taskbar, the app reads the list of apps installed and running on your Mac and your battery level. This information never leaves your Mac.
  • Your settings (pinned apps, options) are saved locally on your Mac only.
  • When you use "Search the web", your search opens in your default web browser, which applies its own privacy policy.

The website

happybarme.com uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking tools. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep technical logs (such as IP addresses) for security purposes, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

HappyNewBar collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.
