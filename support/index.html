Getting started

  • Open the start menu: click the glowing orb in the taskbar, or press ⌥ Option + Space from anywhere.
  • Search: start typing in the start menu to find an app, then press Enter to open the first result. You can also search the web.
  • Pin an app: right-click an app icon (in the taskbar or the start menu) and choose Pin. Choose Unpin to remove it.
  • Switch apps: click an app in the taskbar to bring it to the front. Click it again to minimize it.
  • Show the desktop: click the button at the far right of the taskbar to hide every window.
  • Options: click the HappyNewBar orb in the menu bar at the top of the screen to turn the launch animation or "open at login" on or off, or to quit the app.

Best look: hide the Dock automatically

For the cleanest result, let HappyNewBar take the bottom of your screen:

  1. Open System Settings → Desktop & Dock.
  2. Turn on Automatically hide and show the Dock.

HappyNewBar never changes your system settings by itself.

Frequently asked questions

How do I open HappyNewBar at login?

Check "Open HappyNewBar at login" in the welcome window, or turn it on later from the HappyNewBar orb in the menu bar.

The taskbar covers the Dock. What should I do?

Turn on "Automatically hide and show the Dock" in System Settings → Desktop & Dock (see above).

⌥ Option + Space does nothing.

Another app may already use this shortcut. Quit that app or change its shortcut, then try again. You can always open the start menu by clicking the orb.

Why don't I see the taskbar in a full-screen app?

Like the Dock, the taskbar steps aside when an app is in full screen. Exit full screen to see it again.

Does HappyNewBar collect my data?

No. HappyNewBar runs entirely on your Mac and collects no data. See our Privacy Policy.

Which Macs are supported?

Macs with Apple silicon running macOS 14 or later.

Troubleshooting

  • The taskbar disappeared: open HappyNewBar again from your Applications folder.
  • An app icon is missing: open the start menu, find the app in All apps, right-click it and choose Pin.
  • Something looks wrong after an update: quit HappyNewBar from the menu bar orb and open it again.

Uninstall

  1. Quit HappyNewBar from the orb in the menu bar.
  2. Drag HappyNewBar from your Applications folder to the Trash.
